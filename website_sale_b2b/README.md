SoftMuch e-Commerce B2B
---------------

Optimize sales for retail customers
----------------------------
Optimize your sales to your retail customers, giving them the ability to buy 
quickly and agilely. Your customers will now have the possibility to buy in 
your ecommerce just with a couple of clicks.

Create a new list price for your retail customers
----------------------------
Now you have the possibility to choose the display mode in your ecommerce, 
by selecting the **Retail** version you will have the original Odoo view, 
however by selecting **Wholesale** you will be able to access our incredible 
view so that your retail clients can make their purchases super quickly.

Add products directly from the shop view
----------------------------
Products are added directly from the shop view, simply by clicking on the add 
button or entering the required quantity. Products with variants can be viewed 
by clicking on the grid button.

Grid view to add products to the cart faster
----------------------------
Once the grid button is pressed, a grid opens with all the possible variants. 
You can add the variants to the cart by clicking on the add button or adding the 
desired quantity.

Product view also with a grid
----------------------------
The product view also has a grid view for when a product with variants is selected.
