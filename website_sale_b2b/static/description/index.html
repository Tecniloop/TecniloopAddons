Optimize sales for retail customers

Enhance your sales strategy for retail customers, providing them with the ability to make quick and agile purchases. Your customers can now buy from your e-commerce with just a couple of clicks.



Create a new list price for your retail customers

Choose the display mode in your e-commerce. Selecting the Retail version offers the original Odoo view, while opting for Wholesale provides access to our incredible view for swift retail client transactions.




Add products directly from the shop view

Add products directly from the shop view by clicking the add button or specifying the required quantity. Products with variants can be viewed by clicking the grid button.




Grid view to add products to the cart faster

Pressing the grid button opens a grid with all possible variants. Add variants to the cart by clicking the add button or specifying the desired quantity.




Product view also with a grid

The product view also features a grid view when a product with variants is selected.
