# Website Product Brand

## Overview

The `website_product_brands` enhances the website by allowing you to manage and display product brands in an organized manner. It provides functionality to display brand logos and product associations, making it easier for customers to browse products based on the brand.

### Features:
- Add, update, and remove product brands.
- Display product brands on the website.
- Allow customers to filter products by brand.
- Brand snippet on the home page.
- Enable/Disable the brand filter feature.
- Brands Menu for managing brands directly from the website.

## Prerequisites

- **Odoo Version**: 18

## Configuration

### Configure the product brands

1. Navigate to **Website** in the Odoo backend.
2. Under the **Product Brands** section, Look for **All Brands**.
3. Create the  **Brands**.

## How it Works

On the shop page, customers will see brands that allow them to filter products by brand. The brand snippet will also appear on the home page, and the module provides a separate brands page under the **Brands** menu.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Brands are not showing up on the website.**
**Solution:** Ensure that the brands are created and the created brands will appear in the **Brands** menu.

**Problem: Brands are not showing up in the filter.**
Solution: Make sure that the "Product brand's filter" is enabled and that customer can filter the products based on brands.
